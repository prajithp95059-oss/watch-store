Our story

Stackly began when Ravi Shankar, a self-taught watchmaker, started repairing neighbours' watches on a small counter. Word spread because the work was careful and the advice was honest.

Today we stock over 400 models, from everyday quartz pieces to Swiss automatics, and every watch is checked on our timing machine before it reaches you.
